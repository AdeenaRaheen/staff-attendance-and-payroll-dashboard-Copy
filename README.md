# Staff Attendance & Salary Management Dashboard

## Project overview
A school-administration data management and reporting project built to record monthly staff attendance, calculate attendance-related salary adjustments, and review staff attendance and salary information through Excel and Power BI dashboards.

This project reflects a real administrative workflow: monthly records were maintained in Excel, a month-wise attendance view was created, and the data was then summarised in a Power BI dashboard.

## Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI

## My role
School Administration — data recording, attendance tracking, salary-related record management, Excel reporting, and dashboard development.

## Workflow
1. Recorded monthly staff attendance.
2. Maintained attendance categories including Present, Absent, Leave, and Week Off.
3. Organised monthly staff records in an Excel yearly summary.
4. Used the recorded attendance and salary fields to review paid days, deductions, and total salary.
5. Created month-wise attendance sheets to make daily records easier to review.
6. Built a Power BI dashboard to summarise staff attendance and salary information.

## What the dashboards show
### Excel yearly summary
- Month-wise staff attendance and salary-related fields.
- Present, absent, leave, week-off, and paid-day counts.
- Salary, per-day salary, deductions, and total salary fields.

### Excel monthly attendance sheet
- Daily attendance status for each staff member.
- Month navigation across the year.
- Visual status markers for Present, Absent, Leave, and Week Off.

### Power BI dashboard
- Staff-level attendance summary.
- Present and absent day totals.
- Week-off and leave totals.
- Salary and deduction summary.
- Filters for teacher ID and month.
